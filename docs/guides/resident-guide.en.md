# GrievEase Resident User Guide (English)

## 1. Welcome to GrievEase

GrievEase is your society's private, secure community portal designed for mobile phones and web browsers. It requires no third-party apps, payment gateways, or intrusive trackers.

---

## 2. Getting Started & Account Activation

1. Collect your confidential **Credential Slip** from the society office.
2. Open your mobile browser and navigate to `https://<your-society-domain>/activate`.
3. Enter:
   - Your allocated Username
   - The 10-character Activation Code from the slip
   - Verification Detail (your Flat Number or Date of Birth)
4. Choose a strong, private password (minimum 10 characters).
5. Add GrievEase to your phone's home screen via **Add to Home screen** (PWA).

---

## 3. Submitting Complaints & Grievances

1. Tap **New Grievance** (`/app/grievances/new`).
2. Select Category (Plumbing, Electrical, Security, Noise, Common Area).
3. Set Priority: `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, or `EMERGENCY`.
4. Upload photos from your camera. EXIF location data is automatically stripped.
5. **Anonymous Whistleblower Mode**:
   - Check the **File Anonymously** checkbox if you want to report sensitive committee or neighbor issues.
   - Your name, flat number, and phone number are cryptographically hidden. Even committee members and database administrators cannot see who filed the complaint.
6. Submit and save your unique Grievance Tracking Code (`GR-2026-XXXX`).

---

## 4. Inviting Visitors & Gate Passes

1. Tap **Visitors** (`/app/visitors`).
2. Tap **Create Pass**.
3. Enter visitor name, vehicle number (optional), and expected date/window.
4. Tap **Generate Pass**.
5. Share the generated QR code or 12-character pass token with your guest via messaging.
6. When your guest arrives at the gate, the guard scans the pass for immediate entry.

---

## 5. Amenity Booking

1. Tap **Amenities** (`/app/amenities`).
2. Choose Clubhouse, Gym, Swimming Pool, or Sports Court.
3. Select an available date and time slot.
4. **Hold & Payment**:
   - The slot is reserved for you with a 24-hour hold timer (`AWAITING_PAYMENT`).
   - Visit the society office to pay the booking fee/deposit in cash.
   - Once the office records your cash payment, your booking becomes `CONFIRMED`.
   - Show your digital booking pass to the clubhouse attendant.

---

## 6. Maintenance Bills & Cash Receipts

1. Tap **Billing & Ledger** (`/app/billing`).
2. Inspect your current outstanding dues, breakdown of maintenance charges, and billing statements.
3. **Cash Payments**:
   - Pay maintenance in cash at the society office.
   - The cashier records your payment and gives you an official printed receipt.
   - Your digital ledger balance updates instantly with an immutable credit record.

---

## 7. Working with Vendor Contractors

When the society assigns a contractor (e.g. plumber or electrician) to your complaint:
1. Open the grievance details page.
2. View the assigned contractor details and visit window.
3. **Privacy Protection**: The contractor **never** sees your name or phone number.
4. **Share Number Toggle**: If you want the contractor to call you before arriving, turn on **"Share my number with vendor for this task"**. This permission automatically expires once the job is finished.
5. **Confirming the Job**:
   - After the contractor marks the job complete and uploads the AFTER photo, you will receive an in-app prompt.
   - Tap **"Fixed"** to confirm and give a 1-5 star rating.
   - Or tap **"Not fixed"** to reopen the grievance with a note.

---

## 8. Emergency SOS

In an emergency (medical, fire, security):
1. Tap the red **SOS Emergency** button in the header or menu.
2. Press and hold for 3 seconds (or confirm).
3. The main security gate console will sound an immediate visual and audible alarm with your flat number and wing.

---

## 9. Your DPDP Privacy Rights

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy & DPDP** (`/app/settings/privacy`).
2. **Download My Data**: Export all your personal data, complaint history, visitor logs, and billing statements as a JSON file.
3. **Request Deletion**: Request account closure or data erasure. Your personal details will be pseudonymised while preserving financial audit records.
