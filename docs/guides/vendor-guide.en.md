# GrievEase Vendor Partner Operations Guide (English)

## 1. Overview & Vendor Dashboard

As an approved service contractor (plumbing, electrical, lift maintenance, painting, pest control), you manage assigned maintenance jobs through the **Vendor Portal** (`/vendor`).

---

## 2. Viewing & Accepting Work Orders

1. Log in at `https://<society-domain>/login` using your contractor credentials.
2. The dashboard displays **My Tasks** organized into three tabs:
   - **New**: Newly assigned tasks awaiting your response.
   - **Active**: Tasks accepted or scheduled for visit.
   - **Completed**: Finished tasks with resident ratings.
3. Open a **New** task to review:
   - Issue Title & Description
   - Location: Flat Number and Wing (or Common Area)
   - Preferred Resident Window & Attached Resident Photos
4. **Accept or Decline**:
   - You must **Accept** or **Decline** within the allotted time (default 24 hours).
   - If declining, select a valid reason (e.g. out of parts, capacity full).

---

## 3. Scheduling Visits & Gate Entry

1. After accepting, tap **Schedule Visit**.
2. Pick an estimated date and arrival time.
3. Once scheduled, the system automatically issues an official **Vendor Gate Pass** to your profile.
4. Show your digital gate pass (or provide your company name) to the security guard at the gate for hassle-free entry.

---

## 4. Resident Contact Privacy

- By default, resident names and phone numbers are strictly protected.
- If the resident has chosen to share their number for the specific task, a phone icon will appear: **"Resident Phone (Shared for this task)"**.
- This contact detail expires automatically once the job is marked completed.

---

## 5. Job Execution & Mandatory Photo Proofs

1. When starting work at the site, tap **Start Job**.
2. **Before Photo**: Tap **Upload Before Photo** to take a photo of the broken pipe, wire, or fixture with your phone camera.
3. **After Photo (Mandatory)**:
   - Once repair is complete, tap **Upload After Photo** to take a clear photo of the repaired fixture.
   - The system **requires** an AFTER photo to mark the job complete.
4. Add brief completion notes (e.g. "Replaced 1/2 inch brass valve, tested for leaks").
5. Tap **Mark Completed**.

---

## 6. Resident Sign-Off & Ratings

1. Once marked completed, the resident receives a notification to test the repair and confirm:
   - **"Fixed"**: The job is confirmed. The resident rates your service (1 to 5 stars).
   - **"Not Fixed"**: The job is reopened for inspection.
2. Review your historical rating and resident comments under **Job History** (`/vendor/history`).
